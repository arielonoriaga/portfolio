# Design context

- **Audience:** US founders, CTOs and engineering leads deciding in under a minute whether to reply to a contract engineer.
- **Job:** confirm seniority and fit fast (what he builds, proof, availability), then email or connect.
- **Tone:** a 1930s cartoon short. Title card, intertitles, film grain, rubber-hose motion. Playful frame, serious content; the copy stays plain and factual.
- **Constraints:** static Astro, no JavaScript; motion in CSS/SVG only and off under `prefers-reduced-motion`; original art only (the crab mascot is ours, a nod to Rust); text must stay readable and selectable.
