# Design context

- **Audience:** US founders, CTOs and engineering leads deciding in under a minute whether to reply to a contract engineer.
- **Job:** confirm seniority and fit fast (what he builds, proof, availability), then email or connect.
- **Tone:** an ink-drawn game world. Playful frame, serious content; the copy stays plain and factual.
- **Lineage:** Huggian's landing (cool blue paper, Syne display type, 2px corners, the product graph) plus structures common to games: HUD, achievements, quest log, crafting grid, inventory hotbar, a side-scrolling ground. Inspiration is genre-level only; all art is original.
- **Constraints:** static Astro, no JavaScript; motion in CSS/SVG only and off under `prefers-reduced-motion`; no full-screen animated overlays (they flickered); text must stay readable and selectable.
- **Mascot:** an original rubber-hose crab, a nod to Rust. It walks sideways along the ground, waves, hops and talks.
